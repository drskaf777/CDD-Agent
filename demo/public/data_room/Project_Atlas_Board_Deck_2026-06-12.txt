MERIDIAN DATA SYSTEMS - BOARD MATERIALS
Project Atlas - Strategic Review - 12 June 2026

Slide 1: Internal operating plan, fiscal 2027 to fiscal 2029

The internal plan carries revenue of $584 million in fiscal 2027, growth of 20%. This
is above the $560 million to $572 million range guided to the market on 12 March. The
difference is the governance attach programme, which we have not guided externally
because the pipeline is not yet contracted.

Fiscal 2028 internal plan is $712 million, growth of 22%. Fiscal 2029 is $868 million.

Slide 2: Net revenue retention recovery

The plan assumes net revenue retention recovers to 118% by fiscal 2028 from 112%
today. The recovery is driven by the governance attach programme, which assumes 34%
of the Integration Cloud installed base attaches governance within 24 months. Attach
to date is 9%.

Slide 3: Commercial view versus reported segments

Our two reported segments do not correspond to how we run the business. Internally we
manage four motions: Enterprise Integration ($268 million), Mid-Market Integration
($124 million), Governance Attach ($61 million) and Governance Standalone ($34
million). Mid-Market Integration is where retention has deteriorated: net revenue
retention in that motion was 96% in fiscal 2026.

Slide 4: Pricing

We took a 7% list-price increase in January 2026. Realised price increase was 3.1%
after discounting. Renewal discounting averaged 11% across the mid-market cohort,
against 4% in enterprise.

Slide 5: Public-company cost base

Costs attributable to being listed total $14.7 million annually, of which we estimate
$11.2 million would not be incurred as a private company. The remainder is audit and
insurance that would continue in some form.

Slide 6: Engineering concentration

Fourteen engineers hold primary maintainership of the core transformation engine.
Voluntary turnover in this group was 21% in fiscal 2026 against 14% company-wide.
