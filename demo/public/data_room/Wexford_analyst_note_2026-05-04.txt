SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT - fictional research note, fictional company.

WEXFORD SECURITIES - EQUITY RESEARCH
Meridian Data Systems (MRDN) - Neutral, price target $46
4 May 2026

Estimates. We model fiscal 2027 revenue of $566 million, growth of 16%, in line with
company guidance of $560 million to $572 million. Consensus across the nine brokers
covering the stock sits at $567 million with a range of $558 million to $581 million,
a notably tight dispersion.

We forecast fiscal 2028 revenue of $648 million, growth of 14%, and non-GAAP operating
margin of 18%.

Thesis. Meridian remains the depth leader in enterprise data integration. Our concern
is that the growth algorithm is shifting from expansion to new logos at precisely the
point when the platform vendors are bundling. Net revenue retention of 112% is still
healthy in absolute terms but the three-year trend is the wrong shape.

Valuation. At $41.20 the shares trade on 4.5x our fiscal 2027 revenue estimate, a
discount to the peer median of 5.4x that we regard as warranted given decelerating
retention.

Risks to our view. Faster attach of the governance product; a strategic acquirer
paying for the installed base rather than the growth algorithm.
