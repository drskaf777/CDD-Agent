MERIDIAN DATA SYSTEMS, INC.
Definitive Proxy Statement (Schedule 14A) - filed 30 April 2026

Voting securities and principal holders.

As of the record date there were 62.4 million shares outstanding, comprising 48.7
million shares of Class A common stock carrying one vote per share and 13.7 million
shares of Class B common stock carrying ten votes per share.

Our founder and Chief Executive Officer beneficially owns 13.7 million shares of
Class B common stock, representing 22.0% of shares outstanding and 73.8% of total
voting power. Holders of Class B common stock are therefore able to determine the
outcome of most matters submitted to a vote of stockholders.

Kestrel Partners LP reported beneficial ownership of 5.1% of our Class A common stock
on 14 February 2026 and has publicly called for a review of capital allocation.

Free float, being Class A shares not held by directors, officers or affiliates, was
approximately 68% of shares outstanding.

Governance.

Our certificate of incorporation provides for a classified board divided into three
classes serving staggered three-year terms. Amendment of specified provisions of the
certificate requires the affirmative vote of holders of at least 66 2/3% of total
voting power. A merger or consolidation requires the affirmative vote of a majority
of the total voting power outstanding.

The Board adopted a stockholder rights plan on 3 March 2026 which is triggered at 15%
beneficial ownership by any person or group.

Executive compensation.

Annual incentive compensation for named executive officers is weighted 60% to revenue
growth and 40% to non-GAAP operating margin. No portion is tied to net revenue
retention.
