MERIDIAN DATA SYSTEMS, INC.
Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026 Earnings Call - 12 March 2026

Chief Executive Officer prepared remarks.

We finished fiscal 2026 with revenue of $487.2 million, growth of 19%, which was at
the upper end of the guidance range we gave in December. Integration Cloud grew 21%.

I want to address retention directly. Net revenue retention finished the year at
112%, down from 118%. We guided to 115% at the start of the year and we did not
reach it. The shortfall is concentrated in seat expansion in our mid-market cohort,
where budget scrutiny has been real.

For fiscal 2027 we are guiding revenue of $560 million to $572 million, which is
growth of 15% to 17% at the midpoint. We are guiding non-GAAP operating margin of
16% to 17%.

Chief Financial Officer prepared remarks.

A reminder on our track record: in fiscal 2024 we guided 24% growth and delivered
26%. In fiscal 2025 we guided 21% and delivered 22%. In fiscal 2026 we guided 20%
and delivered 19%, the first year we did not clear our initial guide.

Analyst question and answer.

Analyst: On the platform vendors bundling integration into their agreements - are you
seeing that in competitive deals?

fifth two years ago. Where the customer values depth we still win. Where integration
is a checkbox, we increasingly do not.

CEO: It is the number we believe.
