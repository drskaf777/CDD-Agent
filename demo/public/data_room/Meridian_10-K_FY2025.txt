MERIDIAN DATA SYSTEMS, INC.
Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended 31 January 2026

Item 1. Business

Meridian Data Systems provides cloud data-integration and governance software to
large enterprises. Our platform moves, transforms and governs data across the
systems our customers already run. We sell principally through a direct enterprise
sales force, supported by a partner channel that originated 24% of new bookings in
fiscal 2026.

We report two segments. Integration Cloud generated revenue of $392.0 million in
fiscal 2026, an increase of 21% over the prior year. Data Governance generated
revenue of $95.2 million, an increase of 11%. Total revenue was $487.2 million,
compared with $409.4 million in fiscal 2025, an increase of 19%.

Revenue by geography was United States $321.6 million, Europe $118.4 million, and
rest of world $47.2 million.

No single customer accounted for more than 10% of total revenue in fiscal 2026. Our
ten largest customers represented 21% of total revenue.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Competition. The data-integration market is intensely competitive. The largest cloud
platform vendors have expanded their native integration capabilities and increasingly
bundle them with their broader platform agreements. We expect this bundling to
continue and it may compress our pricing.

Retention. Our dollar-based net revenue retention rate was 112% in fiscal 2026,
compared with 118% in fiscal 2025 and 121% in fiscal 2024. The decline reflects lower
seat expansion within existing accounts. Gross revenue retention was 91%.

Concentration of engineering talent. A significant portion of our platform is
maintained by a small group of engineers, and we have historically experienced
voluntary turnover of approximately 14% annually.

Item 7. Management Discussion and Analysis

Gross margin was 78.1% in fiscal 2026 compared with 79.4% in fiscal 2025. The decline
reflects a higher mix of hosted infrastructure cost as customers migrated to our
managed offering.

Research and development expense was $121.8 million, or 25.0% of revenue. Sales and
marketing expense was $196.4 million, or 40.3% of revenue.

Loss from operations was $18.2 million. On a non-GAAP basis, excluding stock-based
compensation of $84.6 million and restructuring charges of $11.3 million, income from
operations was $77.7 million, representing a non-GAAP operating margin of 15.9%.

Remaining performance obligations were $612.0 million as of 31 January 2026, of which
we expect to recognise approximately 61% within twelve months.
