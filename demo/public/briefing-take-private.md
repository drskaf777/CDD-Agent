Deal team briefing - Project Atlas (take-private)

Target is Meridian Data Systems, Inc., a fictional company used for demonstration,
trading as Meridian, meridiandata.example. Headquartered in Austin, with revenue in
the US (66%), Europe (24%) and rest of world (10%). Publicly traded on NASDAQ under
MRDN. We are acquiring the whole company and taking it private. The offer requires a
shareholder vote and the board's recommendation, and the listing will be cancelled. We are
at early screening and go to IC on 2026-11-20.

Public-market context: ticker MRDN on NASDAQ, currency USD. The unaffected share price
is $41.20 on 2026-04-18, before any market awareness of our interest. There are 62.4
million shares outstanding. Free float is 68%. The founder-CEO holds 22% of shares but
73.8% of voting power through Class B stock, so the structure is dual-class. Nine
brokers cover the stock and published consensus is available. Kestrel Partners is on
the register as an activist. The company is in the Russell 2000. The disclosure
threshold is 5%. A merger would require a majority of total voting power.

Sub-sector is enterprise data-integration and governance SaaS sold to large
enterprises - not horizontal data tooling. Business model is subscription/SaaS,
revenue predominantly recurring, customer base B2B. The company is mature.

The investment thesis in one sentence: take Meridian private at a premium the public
market will not pay itself, and rebuild the growth algorithm away from quarterly
guidance. The model depends
most on two assumptions: that net revenue retention troughs at or above 110% and
recovers, and that governance attach reaches at least 25% of the integration installed
base within 24 months. Base case is 17% organic growth and 700bps of margin expansion
over a five-year hold, exiting to a strategic or sponsor-to-sponsor.

Buyer is a financial sponsor. This would be a new platform investment. What matters
most to our go/no-go: growth optionality, proprietary technology, and cash-flow
stability - in that order.

This is a proprietary situation, bilateral with the company. No other bidders known.
No prior diligence report exists.

Data-room access is by direct file upload and includes confidential management
material, so we should expect to be in possession of material non-public information.
Compliance has recorded the trading restriction and wall-crossed the deal team and the
investment committee. This is an above-the-line process - management is aware. Customer
reference calls are permitted, but no contact with the top-5 customers before signing,
and no direct competitor contact. Industry-expert calls are permitted. Contact with
Meridian insiders or investor relations is not permitted. The NDA requires that
data-room material is purged at engagement close.

Primary audience is the investment committee. We need a slide presentation.
