Deal team briefing - Project Kanpur (take-private)

HYPOTHETICAL MANDATE ON A REAL COMPANY. The target, its filings and the market data
are real and are cited as such. The transaction is not: no approach exists, and the
buyer, thesis and hold assumptions below are a screening exercise constructed to test
the diligence process. Nothing here should be read as reporting an actual deal.

Target is Freshworks Inc., trading as Freshworks, freshworks.com. Headquartered in San
Mateo, California, with substantial engineering operations in Chennai, India. Publicly
traded on NASDAQ under FRSH. We are acquiring the whole company and taking it private.
The offer requires a shareholder vote and the recommendation of the board, and the
listing will be cancelled. We are at early screening and go to IC on 2026-12-11.

Public-market context: ticker FRSH on NASDAQ, currency USD. The reference share price
is $13.86 at close on 2026-08-28. Treat that as the reference price for premium
purposes while noting it is a current market price and not a true unaffected price,
because no approach has been made and nothing has leaked. The 52-week range is $6.79
to $13.99, so the shares sit near the top of that range. There are approximately
260.92 million shares outstanding and market capitalisation is about $3.62 billion.
Fifteen analysts cover the stock and published consensus is available. The share
structure is dual-class: Class A carries one vote and Class B carries ten votes. The
disclosure threshold is 5%.

Sub-sector is customer-experience and IT-service-management SaaS sold to mid-market
businesses - Freshdesk, Freshservice and Freshsales - not horizontal enterprise
software. Business model is subscription/SaaS, revenue predominantly recurring,
customer base B2B. The company is mature and has been public since 2021.

The investment thesis in one sentence: take Freshworks private at a premium the public
market has not paid since 2021, on the view that the mid-market service desk is a
durable franchise being valued as a decelerating one. The model depends most on two
assumptions: that net revenue retention stabilises rather than continuing to erode,
and that AI-native entrants and platform bundling do not compress mid-market pricing
faster than seat and product expansion can offset. Base case is 11% organic growth and
500bps of margin expansion over a five-year hold, exiting to a strategic or
sponsor-to-sponsor.

Buyer is a financial sponsor. This would be a new platform investment, not a bolt-on.
What matters most to our go/no-go: cash-flow stability, growth optionality, and
proprietary technology - in that order.

This is a proprietary situation. No approach has been made and no other bidders are
known. No prior diligence report exists.

Data-room access is by direct file upload. The material we hold today is the public
record only - the annual report, the most recent quarterly report, the proxy statement,
the latest quarterly earnings release, and published analyst consensus. No confidential
management material has been received, so we are not yet in possession of material
non-public information and no trading restriction applies at this stage. This is a
below-the-line process: the company is not aware of our interest, so no outreach that
would reveal the transaction is permitted. No customer contact, no competitor contact,
and no contact with Freshworks insiders or investor relations. Independent
industry-expert calls are permitted provided they do not identify the target. The
retention policy is to purge the engagement index at close.

Primary audience is the investment committee. We need a slide presentation.
