PUBLISHED THIRD-PARTY ANALYSIS - Freshworks Inc. (NASDAQ: FRSH)
Compiled 2026-09-03 from publicly reported aggregates. These are published consensus
figures and market data, not proprietary broker notes: individual sell-side research
is subscription-only and is not reproduced here. Each figure carries its source.

CONSENSUS ESTIMATES

Analysts covering Freshworks forecast revenue of $960.9 million for 2026, across 15
covering analysts. That represents roughly 10% growth on the trailing twelve months.
Source: Simply Wall St, https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/software/nasdaq-frsh/freshworks

Company guidance for 2026 revenue is $952 million to $960 million. Consensus therefore
sits marginally above the top of the guided range.
Source: company guidance as reported alongside consensus, Simply Wall St (above)

MARKET DATA

The shares traded at $13.86 on 2026-08-28, against a 52-week range of $6.79 to $13.99.

near the top of their 52-week range.
Source: Morningstar, https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/xnas/frsh/quote

NOTE ON EVIDENTIAL WEIGHT

Published consensus is not independent corroboration of management guidance. Covering
analysts are guided by the company and build their models from company disclosure, so
agreement between consensus and guidance is substantially one source counted twice.
