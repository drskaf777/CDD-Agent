Deal team briefing - Project Sentinel

Target is Sentinel Secure Ltd, trading as Sentinel, sentinelsecure.example. Headquartered
in Boston, with meaningful revenue in the US (72%), UK (18%), and DACH (10%). Privately
held. We are running a majority buyout. We have a signed LOI and go to IC on 2026-10-15.

Sub-sector is B2B cybersecurity SaaS - specifically cloud workload protection sold to
mid-market enterprises, not horizontal IT-security software. Business model is
subscription/SaaS, revenue is predominantly recurring, customer base is B2B. The company
is scaled but not yet mature.

The investment thesis in one sentence: acquire Sentinel as a platform and cross-sell a
new identity-protection product line into the existing installed base. The model depends
most on two assumptions: that net revenue retention stays at or above 115% through the
hold period, and that at least 30% of the installed base attaches the new product line
within 24 months. Base case is 22% organic growth and 600bps of margin expansion over a
five-year hold, exiting to a strategic or sponsor-to-sponsor.

Buyer is a financial sponsor. This would be a new platform investment, not a bolt-on.
What matters most to our go/no-go: cash-flow stability, growth optionality, and
proprietary technology - in that order.

This is a competitive auction. Two other bidders are believed to be in the process. A
sell-side vendor due diligence report exists and we should reconcile against it rather
than duplicate it.

Data-room access is by direct file upload. Management is aware diligence is underway.
Customer reference calls are permitted, but no contact with the top-5 customers before
signing, and no direct competitor contact at all. Industry-expert calls are permitted.
The NDA requires that data-room material is purged at engagement close.

Primary audience is the investment committee. We need a slide presentation.
