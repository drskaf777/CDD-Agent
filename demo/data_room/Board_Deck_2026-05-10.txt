Sentinel Secure Ltd - Board Deck - May 2026

Slide 1: Business Review Q1 2026
Net revenue retention was 118% for the trailing twelve months. Gross revenue retention
was 91%. ARR closed the quarter at $48.2m, up from $39.4m a year earlier. Management
regards the retention figure as sustainable through the plan period.

Slide 2: Customer Base
We ended the quarter with 412 customers. The top 5 customers represent 31% of ARR and
the top 20 represent 54%. Concentration has risen from 26% two years ago as the
enterprise segment has grown faster than mid-market. Logo churn was 9% for the year.

Slide 3: Growth Drivers
Growth is driven by seat expansion in existing accounts and by new logo acquisition in
the mid-market segment. Blended CAC payback is 19 months, lengthening from 15 months in
2024 as competition for mid-market accounts has intensified. Gross margin is 78%,
down 200bps year on year as support headcount was added.

Slide 4: Product and Roadmap
The identity-protection module is in limited availability with 11 design-partner
accounts. General availability is planned for Q4 2026. Attach rate among design partners
is 64%, though these accounts were selected for fit and are not a representative sample
of the installed base.

Slide 5: Market
Management estimates the addressable market for cloud workload protection in our served
segments at $6.1bn, growing at 21% annually. This estimate is derived from a 2024
analyst report scaled forward; it has not been rebuilt bottom-up from account counts.

Slide 6: Risks
Two of our five largest accounts are up for renewal in FY27. Pricing pressure from
platform vendors bundling workload protection is increasing in the mid-market. Two
senior engineers who own the detection pipeline are not on retention agreements.
