Sentinel Secure Ltd - Master Subscription Agreements - Top 20 Accounts - Extract
Prepared 2026-03-15 for diligence purposes.

Section 1. Scope
This extract consolidates the commercial terms of the twenty largest subscription
agreements by annual contract value as at 31 March 2026.

Section 2. Term and Renewal
2.1 Each Agreement has an initial term of thirty-six (36) months, renewing automatically
for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives ninety (90) days
written notice of non-renewal.

2.2 In eleven (11) of the twenty Agreements, representing approximately 25% of total
annual recurring revenue, the subscription fee for each renewal term is subject to a
contractual step-down. The step-down reduces the per-seat rate by between eight percent
(8%) and fifteen percent (15%) at first renewal, reflecting volume commitments made at
initial signature. The blended net revenue retention figure reported to the board does
not isolate the effect of these step-downs.

2.3 In the remaining nine (9) Agreements, the subscription fee is subject to an annual
escalator of the lesser of CPI or four percent (4%).

Section 3. Expansion and Cross-Sell
3.1 Additional modules, including any identity-protection module, are licensed under a
separate order form and are not included in the base subscription fee.

3.2 Three (3) Agreements contain a most-favoured-pricing clause under which any new
module must be offered at no worse than the pricing offered to comparable customers.

Section 4. Termination
4.1 Customer may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days notice in two (2)
Agreements, together representing approximately 7% of annual recurring revenue.

Section 5. Renewal Calendar
5.1 Of the twenty Agreements, six (6) reach first renewal during calendar year 2027,
including two of the five largest accounts by annual contract value. Those two accounts
together represent approximately 17% of annual recurring revenue and both carry the
step-down provision described in clause 2.2.
